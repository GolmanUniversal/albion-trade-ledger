Starter plan, 0 to 1M silver

  1. Gather T3–T4 in blue and yellow zones. Check the Gathering tab for which raw resource pays best right now, then gather that one. Selling raw is fine at this stage.
  2. Refine what you gather in that resource's bonus city. Ore in Thetford, hide in Martlock, fiber in Lymhurst, wood in Fort Sterling, stone in Bridgewatch. The Refining tab shows whether refining beats selling raw today.
  3. Turn on focus when you have Premium. Refining with focus in the bonus city returns about half your materials. It is the most reliable early profit.
  4. Craft one item line in its bonus city. Use the Crafting tab, filter to your tier, and pick items that show profit and sell every day (open an item to see its daily sales).
  5. Flip between royal cities only. Keep the risk setting on Safe until your silver passes about 1M. Then read the Risk tab before your first Caerleon run.
  6. Log your silver once a day in the Tracker. After a week you will see which method actually paid.

What the numbers assume

    Refining profit

    One unit of refined output. Material cost is reduced by the resource return rate. “Quick sale” fills an existing buy order. “Listed” posts a sell order at the current lowest price and waits.

    Crafting profit

    Normal quality, no enchantment. Crafted in the item's bonus city. Inputs bought at the lowest price in that city. Artifacts are never returned. Listings priced more than double the typical price elsewhere are ignored as outliers. Click a row for daily sales history.

    Flipping

    Buy from the lowest sell order in one city, sell in another. Your risk setting filters routes. Profit is per unit, after tax and fees. “Units” is how many you can buy with a quarter of your silver, capped at one stack (999) because the price service cannot see how many are actually for sale. Open a row to check daily sales before you commit.

    What to gather

    Best price for each raw resource across the royal cities and Brecilien. “Per kg” matters because your bag and mount weight is the real limit on a gathering trip.
